A college degree is far from obligatory for those who aspire to own a business. Big companies like Microsoft (Bill Gates), Canva (Melanie Perkins) and Stripe (John and Patrick Collison) have thrived despite their founders dropping out of college before completing their studies. And these are just some of the biggest names.
